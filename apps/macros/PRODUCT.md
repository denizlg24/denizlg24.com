# Macros recipe publishing

Macros tracks nutrition and keeps reusable recipes. A recipe can also be an
article: a title, a one-line subtitle, a cover photo, a free-form story and
ordered steps, each of which can carry text, headings, lists, quotes and
captioned photos. The food icon and cover photo have separate roles.

Recipes are written in the native app or in the web kitchen at
`macros.denizlg24.com/kitchen`, with the same account; both edit the same
recipe, ingredients and amounts included. Recipes are private by default.
Publishing makes one available in the public `/recipes` browse and at
`/recipes/<id>`. Readers cook from the article without signing in.
Unpublishing, archiving or suspending the owner removes public access. Photos
stay in private object storage and are served through visibility checks.

The look is Medium-like editorial on top of the existing Macros identity:
monochrome light/dark tokens, hairlines instead of boxes, Inter for titles,
headings and controls, Source Serif for reading text (18–20 px, 1.6 line
height) in a 680 px column, photography wider than the column. The browse page
is a feed — title, two-line subtitle, date and time, thumbnail on the right.
The kitchen is a quiet writing surface: a borderless title and subtitle, the
story, then details, ingredients and method, with a selection toolbar and a
"+" insert menu. Preserve this identity when adding recipe surfaces.
