# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js and shadcn/ui, packaged for Deniz Cloud Forge.

## Users

The primary user is the requester's father. The app brings his daily market monitoring, portfolio tracking and company research into one workspace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Explore markets through charts, heat maps, symbol search and plain-language requests.
- Keep watchlists, transactions, research and preferences with the signed-in Deniz Cloud account.
- Show the source, timestamp and currency of market data.
- Start with an empty dashboard and let requests build it. Offer examples that cover market groups, portfolios and research. Keep US and broad European markets available from Markets.
